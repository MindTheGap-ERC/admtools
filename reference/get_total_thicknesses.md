# get thicknesses of sections from depth-depth curves

get thicknesses of sections from depth-depth curves

## Usage

``` r
get_total_thicknesses(x, ...)
```

## Arguments

- x:

  a depth-depth curve (ddc object)

- ...:

  other parameters (currently unused)

## Value

a numeric vector of length 2 - the thicknesses of section 1 and 2 in the
depth-depth curve

## See also

- [`get_total_thickness()`](https://mindthegap-erc.github.io/admtools/reference/get_total_thickness.md)
  to determine the total thickness accumulated by age-depth models or
  sediment accumulation curves.
